Before you roll

Tolls & vignettes

Italy charges motorway tolls as you go. Austria needs a vignette plus a separate Brenner Pass toll, and Czechia needs an e-vignette — buy both online before crossing. Germany has no car tolls; Poland is mostly free.

Pack for two climates

Through Jun 30 it's hot and sunny — breathable layers, sun cover, water in the car, early starts on the Regensburg and Český Krumlov days. From Jul 1 it turns cooler and showery: keep a light rain shell and one warm layer on top of the bags for the Baltic stretch.

Book ahead

Český Krumlov and Dresden fill up in early July. Reserve those soon. Český Krumlov's castle interior is closed Mondays — you arrive Monday, so tour it Tuesday morning before leaving.

Re-check the back half

The Jul 1–3 end of the forecast is still a week out and will shift. Look again midweek for Dresden, Szczecin, and the Kołobrzeg arrival, and leave Szczecin by ~8:30 AM on the 3rd for a comfortable cushion before noon.
